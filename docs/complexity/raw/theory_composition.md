=========== (b) P1' — MINIMAL MISSING GADGET ===========
TARGET THEOREM (proposed): OFFLINE-HD-BAG clearing (and survival) under skyline hard drop is NP-complete at generalized (n,m). Resolves FUN 2024's open problem (p.23) in the hard-drop model, complementing FUN 2026's SRS-only resolution.

KEY OBSERVATION 1 [PROVEN-trivial]: in P1' the reduction designer CHOOSES the sequence including within-bag order (bag-legality is the only constraint). Order-robustness — the hardest property of FUN 2026's absorber — is NOT required for P1'.

KEY OBSERVATION 2 [PROVEN, machine-verified this session — see proofs MV1–MV6]: junk absorption is NOT the obstacle. Every one of the 7 piece types has a dedicated constant-width "well" with a periodic hole-free skyline self-tiling: O (width 2, period 1, flush), I (width 4 horizontal, period 1, flush; or width-1 vertical), L and J (width 4, period 2, flush), T (width 4, period 4, flush — pinwheel order T-rot2@c0, T-rot1@c2, T-rot3@c0, T-rot0@c1), S and Z (width 2, period 1, monotone 1-step staircase, never flush, requires one prefilled garbage cell). Additionally [PROVEN]: S and Z have NO hole-free placement on flat ground (helps forcing), and two T's can NOT flush-fill 4×2 or 2×4 (T-checkerboard-parity, so any per-bag stratum-clearing design is parity-blocked — use the no-clear architecture instead).

ARCHITECTURE [ARGUED]: Temprano's global frame: full-height walls isolate wells and buckets; one empty 1-wide fill column gates ALL row clears until a terminal I-drain phase; exact global cell budget (supply = empty space below the line) makes any hole immediately fatal for the CLEARING objective. Wells grow at fixed deterministic rates (24 junk cells/bag); board height poly(#bags) — fine since 3-PARTITION is strongly NP-complete. Terminal phase: S/Z staircases end with a 1-step defect — give each S/Z well a private 1-wide drain column completed by tail-bag I's; pad bag count to ≡0 mod 4 so L/J/T wells end flush.

THE SINGLE MISSING GADGET [ARGUED]: a RATE-MATCHED CHOICE gadget. Temprano's digit unit consumes 2 T's + 1 L per unit — incompatible with 1-of-each-type-per-bag supply. Two candidate fixes: (i) redesign the digit gadget for 7-periodic supply (each unit consumes exactly one bag's core allotment); (ii) an I-driven core (FUN-2024-style unary filling: one vertical I per bag into the player-chosen open bucket), with bucket open/close performed by the bag's designated piece (e.g., J) during dedicated "phase bags". The residual proof obligation, and the only genuinely new work: PHASE-DISCIPLINE FORCING — during filling bags the opener piece must have no gap-free placement outside its well, and during phase bags the well slot must be blocked or globally fatal by the exact budget. This is a finite family of surface-enumeration lemmas, machine-checkable in our engine exactly like MV1–MV6 (Temprano's Lemmas 5–10 are the same genre).

WHY THE S/Z WORRY DISSOLVES [PROVEN + ARGUED]: S/Z never touch flat tunnel floors — they nest hole-free on their own staircase (MV2); holes/overhangs are created only if misrouted, which the exact budget makes fatal — i.e., S/Z's flat-hole property is an ASSET (forcing), not an obstacle. Renewability is not needed because the absorber never clears (no-clear gate); only EXACT TERMINAL FILLABILITY is needed, which the drains provide.

SURVIVAL VARIANT [ARGUED]: with zero clears, survival ⇔ total wasted cells ≤ m·n − prefill − 4·(#pieces); choose m so the intended (zero-waste) play fits exactly and any single hole overflows within the appended bag-legal flood. VERDICT: plausible-with-work; no new complexity-theoretic idea required.

=========== (c) P2 — ONLINE HORIZON, PSPACE STRATEGY ===========
MEMBERSHIP [PROVEN]: P2 ∈ PSPACE (poly-size state, unary horizon, alternating game → depth-N minimax in poly space; APTIME=PSPACE).
NP-HARDNESS [ARGUED, near-corollary of (b)]: type-dedicated wells see only their own type's per-bag singleton, so within-bag order is INVISIBLE to them — order-robust absorption is automatic. Making the choice/phase gadget order-robust within its bag upgrades the P1' construction to P2 NP-hardness (adversary's order freedom nullified).
PSPACE SKETCH: alternation ∀(piece draw from bag multiset)/∃(placement) is exactly QBF-shaped; adversary has log2(5040)≈12.3 order bits per bag, Θ(N) bags — raw alternation is ample. Primitives needed: (1) ORDER-DETECTOR — region shared by exactly two designated types where arrival order leaves a distinguishable height-offset pattern; both orders hole-free playable; totality over all 5040 orders (every order parses as some bit). Skyline-constructible in principle (no kicks needed; height offsets are natively readable: a later drop lands flush vs buries a hole depending on recorded offset). (2) ∃-choice: Temprano open-bucket (exists). (3) EVALUATION: with the no-clear discipline, survive-N ⇔ waste ≤ budget — an exact-packing GAME; formula-false must force ≥1 extra wasted cell. Clean, avoids clear mechanics. INFORMATION TIMING IS NOT AN OBSTACLE [ARGUED]: the player seeing the piece before placing is the standard ∀-then-∃ move order; commitment is forced by the fatal-hole budget, and the next ∀ bit arrives only after the placement commits — exactly QBF alternation.
THE REAL OBSTACLE [ARGUED]: ∀-BIT FAN-OUT. A piece is placed once; a bit recorded in one detector cannot be copied across walls (no piece traversal under skyline). QBF needs variables read in multiple clauses. Driving copies from different bags lets the adversary set copies INCONSISTENTLY, changing the game value. Fixes to explore: bounded-occurrence PSPACE-complete QBF variants + an inconsistency-comparator gadget that rewards the player (waste credit) when copies disagree; or reduce from a read-once-flavored PSPACE-complete game (Stockmeyer–Schaefer family). This comparator is the needs-new-idea kernel.
HONEST VERDICT: NP-hardness of P2 = plausible-with-work (small delta on (b)). PSPACE-hardness = plausible-with-work-plus-one-new-idea (the fan-out/consistency gadget); not hopeless — the waste-budget framing and automatic order-robust wells remove the two obstacles that look scariest at first sight.

=========== (d) P3 — INFINITE HORIZON, EXPTIME? ===========
MEMBERSHIP [PROVEN]: P3 ∈ EXPTIME (safety game on ≤ 2^{nm}·2^7·7 explicit states; gfp attractor computation linear in graph size).
P2↔P3 PADDING [PROVEN, standard]: Safe_k (can guarantee surviving k pieces) is monotone shrinking and stabilizes at k* ≤ #states, so survive-forever ⇔ survive 2^{Θ(nm)} pieces. This is NOT a poly reduction either way: P3→P2 needs an exponential (binary) horizon, P2 is defined with unary N (and P2∈PSPACE while P3's natural class is EXPTIME — the gap is real, matching chess/checkers-style succinct safety games). P2→P3 would need a "haven": after simulating N steps, the board must reach a state PROVABLY survivable forever.
THE PROJECT-SPECIFIC BLOCKER [ARGUED — the sharpest observation in this section]: any P3 hardness reduction must contain YES-branches, i.e., gadget states from which ADVERSARIAL forever-survival is provable. In our model no such state has ever been proven to exist — that is precisely the Atlas project's open M2 milestone (cooperative survivability is proven in Lean; adversarial is open). So P3 lower bounds are GATED BEHIND M2: without a certified adversarial closed cycle to serve as the haven/accept-state, no reduction can certify its intended play. Conversely, if the adversarial safe set were empty at all dimensions, P3 would be trivially decidable (always NO). P3's hardness status is thus entangled with the truth of the Atlas conjecture itself.
WHAT EXPTIME-HARDNESS WOULD ADDITIONALLY NEED [ARGUED]: (i) RENEWABLE forced gadgets — structures reset via clears infinitely often; no published Tetris reduction has any (all are one-shot with clears gated to a terminal phase); (ii) board-as-tape encoding with placement+clear as tape writes (capacity fine: nm bits); (iii) all board writes are PLAYER moves — the adversary never touches the board, so ∀-influence must be laundered through piece-identity-forced player responses (possible in principle, no precedent). VERDICT: SPECULATIVE; the right conjecture shape (succinct-state safety game = canonical EXPTIME-complete profile), but premature until M2 is settled; expect years, not months.
P4 [ARGUED]: no meaningful hardness theory exists or is possible in the usual sense — input is two integers (sparse/unary-like language; NP-hardness would collide with Mahaney-style sparse-language results under unary encoding); each instance is a single finite decidable statement (EXPTIME in nm uniformly, O(1) nonuniformly). P4 is mathematics — the Atlas program itself — not complexity theory.

## confidence_notes

Most delicate points: (1) The entire matrix hinges on the equivalence "hard-drops-only with empty top rows = skyline drop" — I confirmed the engine side at source level (heights-only landing computation), and the paper side rests on the digests' explicit quotes; I rate the equivalence itself near-certain, the per-construction applications ~90-95%. (2) The (b) verdict "plausible-with-work, no new theory" is the strongest editorial judgment in this report; the identified crux (rate-matched choice + phase forcing) is exactly where such constructions historically fail — treat timelines skeptically, but note that FAILURE modes are all detectable cheaply by the same machine-checking harness used here, so the direction is low-cost to falsify. (3) The T-parity analysis (why per-bag stratum clearing fails and the no-clear architecture is forced) is machine-confirmed at the 2-T scale (MV4) and consistent with the project's Lean PieceCharge result, but the general "any stratum design is parity-blocked" claim is ARGUED for single-bag periods only; multi-bag-period clearing strata are not ruled out (they're just unnecessary). (4) The P3 "gated behind M2" observation is an argument about PROOF STRATEGY (reductions need certifiable YES-branches), not a formal barrier theorem — a sufficiently clever reduction might synthesize its haven inside the proof; I consider that unlikely but cannot exclude it. (5) The S self-repro well needs its initial 1-step garbage AND (in a real construction) a way to terminally drain a never-flush staircase; the drain design (private I-column) is sketched, unverified. (6) All "OPEN" verdicts (e.g., 2-piece non-I alphabets in skyline, one-piece skyline) mean unproven-in-either-direction; FUN 2026's positive Thm 3.1 hints some I-only skyline families are easy, so hardness conjectures there should be made with care.
